\subsection{Location and Dataset}

In this project, the range of methodologies previously described in this section are focused into a
single pipeline and applied to a real-world dataset.

\subsubsection{Taï National Park}

Taï National Park is a protected World Heritage conservation site of great importance, located
in Ivory Coast, West Africa.\cite{whs}
It is one of only few significant rainforested areas remaining in the region, covering an area
of roughly 4500 $\mathrm{km}^\mathrm{2}$.\cite{tnp_deforestation}
It has a rich biodiversity; however, populations face persistent threat from predominantly
human-induced factors.\cite{wcf_conservation,anti_poaching}, highlighting the urgency for
improved and novel methods of biomonitoring.

\subsubsection{Chimpanzee Dataset}

The dataset, supplied and sourced by the Wild Chimpanzee Foundation,\cite{wcf} contains large
volume unprocessed raw camera trap video capturing chimpanzees at a total of 65 camera trap transects.
